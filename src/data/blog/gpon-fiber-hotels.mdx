## What Is GPON?

Gigabit Passive Optical Network (GPON) is a fiber-based networking technology that delivers high-speed connectivity through optical fiber.

## Why Hotels Use GPON

Hotels benefit from:

- Faster internet speeds
- Reduced cabling requirements
- Lower maintenance costs
- Better scalability

## GPON vs Traditional Ethernet

Traditional networks require large amounts of copper cabling and active equipment.

GPON simplifies infrastructure by using passive optical splitters.

## Benefits For Hospitality

GPON can support:

- Guest Wi-Fi
- IPTV
- CCTV
- VoIP
- Smart hotel systems

## Conclusion

GPON provides hotels with a future-ready infrastructure designed for modern hospitality demands.
