## What Is PMS Integration?

A Property Management System (PMS) connects hotel operations into one centralized platform.

## Systems Hotels Can Integrate

- Door locks
- IPTV
- Payment systems
- Booking engines
- Guest communication platforms

## Benefits

Integration reduces manual work and improves accuracy.

## Conclusion

Connected hotel technology creates smoother operations and better guest experiences.
